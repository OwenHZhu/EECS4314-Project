# EECS4314-Project

## About BookAtlas

BookAtlas is a dynamic, database-driven book tracking platform that allows users to engage with books they've read, are currently reading, or plan to read in the future. Upon registering and logging in, users can build a personal library by adding books and assigning each one a status (read, currently reading, dropped, finished), as well as rate each book out of five stars. Users can further organize their books through Wishlist and Favourites sections, making it easy to track titles of interest and highlight standout reads. Each user maintains a public profile reflecting their library, ratings, and reading history.

BookAtlas also plans to introduce a community discussion forum designed to bring readers together around the books and genres they care about most. Forums are organized by individual book and by genre, giving users a structured space to dive deep into specific titles or explore broader literary conversations. Within each forum, threads are categorized into **Spoilers**, **Questions**, and **Theories**, keeping discussions organized, accessible, and respectful of readers at different stages of a book. Users can create threads, post replies, and engage in ongoing conversations with fellow readers about the books they love.
